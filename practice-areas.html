Areas of Practice

Areas of practice and legal guides

The areas of law in which the chamber practises, each with a plain-language guide to the governing law, the procedure and the leading court decisions.

01

Motor Accident Claims (MACT)

Claims for compensation under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 for death, injury or damage to property in road accidents.

  • Claims for death and for injury, including permanent disability
  • Fixed compensation under Section 164 and hit-and-run claims
  • Insurer’s defences such as licence and permit violations
Read the MACT guide
02

Insurance Claims & Disputes

Disputes over the rejection, delay or short settlement of life, health, motor and property insurance claims.

  • Repudiation for non-disclosure or pre-existing disease
  • Insurance Ombudsman and Consumer Commission remedies
  • Nomination and the rights of legal heirs
Read the insurance guide
03

Property & Land

Civil disputes over immovable property and compensation when land is acquired by the State.

  • Title, possession and injunction suits
  • Partition of family property and succession
  • Land acquisition compensation under the 2013 Act
Read the property guide
04

Consumer Protection

Complaints under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 before the District, State and National Commissions.

  • Defective goods and deficiency in service
  • Builder delays, banking, insurance and medical services
  • Product liability and e-commerce
Read the consumer guide
05

Labour & Employment

Rights of workers and obligations of employers under the four Labour Codes, in force since 21 November 2025.

  • Termination, retrenchment and industrial disputes
  • Wages, gratuity and social security
  • Employee’s compensation for workplace injuries
Read the labour guide
06

Family & Matrimonial Law

Matrimonial proceedings under the personal laws and related remedies before the Family Court and Magistrate courts.

  • Divorce by mutual consent and contested divorce
  • Maintenance, custody and guardianship
  • Protection and residence orders under the Domestic Violence Act
Read the family law guide
About these guides

The guides set out the law as it stood when they were last reviewed (October 2026). The law changes through new legislation and court decisions, and the outcome of any case depends on its own facts. The guides are for general information and are not legal advice. See the legal updates for recent changes.
