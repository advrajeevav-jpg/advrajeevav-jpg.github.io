Privacy policy

This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

Information collected by this website

This website does not have contact forms, user accounts, advertising or analytics tracking, and it does not ask you for personal information.

To remember that you have accepted the disclaimer, a single entry is saved in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device, is not sent to anyone and contains no personal information. You can remove it by clearing your browser’s site data.

Third-party services

Some parts of the website are provided by third parties, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when the page loads:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website;
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces;
  • Google Maps, which displays the location map and may set cookies; and
  • Google Translate, which provides the optional Malayalam translation.

The use of information by these services is governed by their own privacy policies.

When you contact the chamber

If you telephone or email the chamber, the information you provide is used only to respond to you and, if a matter is taken up, to act in it. It is kept confidential, consistent with an advocate’s professional obligations, and is not shared except as the law requires.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated from time to time. For any question about it, write to advavrajeev.in@gmail.com.
