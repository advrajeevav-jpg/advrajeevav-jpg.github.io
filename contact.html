Before a meeting

Please bring copies of all documents that relate to the matter, such as notices, agreements, policy papers, medical records, title deeds, earlier court orders and correspondence, arranged by date. A legal opinion can be given only after the documents have been reviewed.

Please note

Legal opinions are not given over the telephone or by email without a review of the documents. Contacting the chamber by telephone or email does not by itself create an advocate–client relationship. Please do not send confidential information until you have spoken to the chamber.
