Recent changes in the law

Important legislative changes and court decisions of 2024–2025, with what they mean in practice.

All legal updates
  • 21 Nov 2025

    Four Labour Codes come into force

    The Code on Wages, the Industrial Relations Code, the Code on Social Security and the OSH Code replace 29 central labour laws, including the Industrial Disputes Act and the Payment of Gratuity Act.

    What changed
  • 4 Nov 2025

    Supreme Court: accident claims not to be dismissed as time-barred

    While challenges to the six-month limit in Section 166(3) of the Motor Vehicles Act are pending, Tribunals and High Courts have been directed not to reject claims on the ground of delay.

    Read more
  • Jul 2025

    Kerala High Court: daughters’ equal rights in ancestral property

    Sections 3 and 4 of the Kerala Joint Hindu Family System (Abolition) Act, 1975 were held to be repugnant to Section 6 of the Hindu Succession Act, as amended in 2005, and so cannot prevail over it.
