Practice

Adv. A V Rajeev enrolled with the Bar Council of Kerala in 1993 and has practised at Kozhikode (Calicut) since then. The practice is mainly in civil matters before the civil courts, the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal, the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, the Family Court and other tribunals at Kozhikode, and before the High Court of Kerala.

Areas of practice

  • Motor accident compensation claims
  • Insurance claims and disputes
  • Property, land and partition matters, and land acquisition compensation
  • Consumer complaints
  • Labour and employment matters
  • Family and matrimonial matters

How matters are handled

A matter is taken up only after the relevant documents have been reviewed. Clients are told what the law provides, how the proceedings are likely to unfold and what each stage involves, so that decisions are taken with full information. Meetings at the chamber are by prior appointment.

Legal information on this website

The legal guides and legal updates on this website explain, in plain language, the law and procedure in each area of practice and the leading decisions of the Supreme Court and the High Courts. They are written for general awareness and are reviewed periodically. They are not a substitute for advice on the facts of a particular case.
